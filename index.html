How it works

One split, three models

About 80% of rows are used for fitting or as labeled examples. The three models predict the remaining 20%, whose outcome values are used to score the predictions. Classification keeps each class represented in both sets.

Models

Kumo Tabular Small predicts from labeled examples. Random Forest combines 64 trees. The traditional model is lightly regularized linear or logistic regression, with one-vs-rest logistic regression for multiple classes.

Dataset

IBM’s HR Attrition & Performance dataset describes 1,470 fictional employees. Use it to explore prediction, with no inference about intervention effects or real employees.
